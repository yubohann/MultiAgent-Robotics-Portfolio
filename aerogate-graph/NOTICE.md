# Notice

**AeroGate Graph** is a research-stage project. The associated paper is in
preparation and has **not** been published.

To protect the novelty of the work, this repository distributes the **method
framework** only:

- the problem formulation and research questions,
- the architecture and pipeline design,
- neutral engineering primitives,
- the public API surface of each research component.

The following are **withheld** and released together with the paper:

- the graph encoder / policy architecture,
- reward design and shaping terms,
- training schedule, curriculum rules, and checkpoint-selection logic,
- the expert-guidance mechanism and route-planning internals,
- all experimental configuration values and quantitative results.

If you are an academic supervisor or collaborator and require access to the
full implementation, contact the author directly.
