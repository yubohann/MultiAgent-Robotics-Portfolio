# Notice

**FraudGraph ML Engineering** is a research-stage project. The associated
paper is in preparation and has **not** been published.

To protect the novelty of the work, this repository distributes the **method
framework** only:

- the research questions and protocol,
- the architecture and engineering discipline,
- dependency-light engineering utilities,
- the public API surface of each research component.

The following are **withheld** and released together with the paper:

- the graph encoder and sequence encoder designs,
- the fusion classifier and multi-objective training,
- the federated-style controller and dataset scheduler,
- dataset adapter feature engineering,
- all experimental configuration values and quantitative results.

Dataset files, generated graphs, checkpoints, and experiment outputs remain
local by design and are never redistributed by this repository.

If you are an academic supervisor or collaborator and require access to the
full implementation, contact the author directly.
