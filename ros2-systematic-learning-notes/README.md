# ROS 2 Systems Engineering Notes

[English](README.md) | [中文](README.zh-CN.md)

**A structured ROS 2 engineering handbook covering fundamentals, communication, TF2, simulation, Nav2, MoveIt 2, DDS, performance, embedded deployment, fault tolerance, cloud robotics and current robotics tooling.**

The notes follow the work of a ROS 2 development engineer across 18 chapters and hundreds of sections, from workspaces, topics, services and actions through QoS, coordinate transforms, navigation, motion planning, containerized deployment, multi-robot coordination and performance tuning. The chapters include 2024 to 2026 frontier material, including LLM navigation, VLA and VLM integration, 3D Gaussian splatting, diffusion policies and world models.

**Status.** Maintained as a living handbook, with the full PDF and the chapter map retained in this directory.

## Contents

- ROS 2 workspaces, nodes, topics, services, actions, parameters and lifecycle
- DDS and QoS, TF2, URDF and Xacro, Gazebo, Nav2, MoveIt 2 and embedded deployment
- Performance profiling, fault tolerance, security, cloud robotics and frontier tooling
